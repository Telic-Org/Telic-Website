Fair questions

Things people ask us first.

Isn't this just RPA with AI sprinkled on top?

No. RPA follows a script someone wrote in advance and stops when anything on screen changes. Telic is given the goal and the rules, works out the steps itself, and keeps the ones that work. When something changes, it re-learns that one step instead of breaking.

Why not just point an AI agent at the screen?

Fine for a one-off. At thousands of cases a month, paying an AI to rethink every click is slow, expensive, and the results drift from run to run. Telic spends AI where it earns its keep and uses memory everywhere else.

Do our systems need APIs?

No. Telic works the screens the way your team does. If an API exists for part of the job, it can use that too.

Where does our data go?

Telic runs on your own machines, where the work already happens. For sensitive work, the cloud side can run in your private cloud or on-prem.

What happens when it isn't sure?

It stops safely and asks. Anyone can watch a run live, take the mouse, fix the case and hand it back. Telic checks the result before it carries on.

How do we get started?

Pick one high-volume workflow. Our engineers set Telic up with your team, on your machines, and prove it on real cases before anything else gets automated.

How is it priced?

You can try it free on sample workflows. For real work there's a one-time setup, then a monthly fee plus usage — and because remembered steps cost almost nothing to replay, the work gets cheaper as it runs.
