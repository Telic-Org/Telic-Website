    Why it's different

    Three things a script can't do and an agent won't.

    Scripts are cheap until something changes. AI agents adapt, but they rethink every click and leave you guessing why they did what they did. Telic is built for the gap in between.

    01 / 03

    It remembers.

    It works a step out once, then replays it. Thinking is saved for what's actually new — so the thousandth case is faster and cheaper than the first.
